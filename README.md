## Hi, I'm Yatharth Jaggi

Data Science student at **Purdue University** (Class of 2030). I'm interested in
machine learning, AI agents, and using public data to model real-world systems,
from satellites falling out of orbit to games that build themselves.

### What I'm working on
- **Air Quality Alert**: a Java command-line tool that automatically detects your
  location, pulls live air quality data from the Open-Meteo API, and tells you in
  plain words whether it's a good day to head outside, with a warning when the AQI
  crosses a level you choose
- **Satellite Reentry Prediction**: machine learning on tens of thousands of
  historical reentries and solar-activity data to predict how long objects stay in
  low Earth orbit, plus a daily pipeline collecting live orbit snapshots
- **HealthCareProject**: a group research project analyzing how healthcare has
  changed over time
